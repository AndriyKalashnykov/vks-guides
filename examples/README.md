# The kubeconfig scripts on this box: what each creates and how they differ

The administrator runs these, except kubeconfig-sso.sh / kubeconfig-sso-insecure.sh / kubeconfig-sso-plugin.sh, which a
person runs with their own SSO password. [docs/KUBECONFIG.md](../docs/KUBECONFIG.md) lists every kubeconfig file the guides make.

| | kubeconfig-sso.sh / kubeconfig-sso-insecure.sh | kubeconfig-sso-plugin.sh | kubeconfig-cert.sh | kubeconfig-serviceaccount.sh | rolebinding-idp-group.sh + kubeconfig-idp-group.sh | kubeconfig-builtin-admin.sh |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **creates** | a kubeconfig for SSO_USER in VKS_CLUSTER (with / without the vCenter CA) | the same, through the kubectl vSphere plugin (deprecated) | a certificate kubeconfig for one person | a ServiceAccount token kubeconfig | the group's rights (RoleBinding, or ClusterRoleBinding with `"*"`) + the group's shared kubeconfig | the cluster's built-in admin kubeconfig |
| **identity** | the person's vCenter SSO account | the person's vCenter SSO account | just a name in a certificate (`PERSON-<date>`); nothing is created for it in the cluster | a real object inside the cluster: ServiceAccount `cicd-account` in `kubeconfig-access` | each person's account in the identity provider, in a group | the cluster's own administrator, shared by every holder |
| **what the file holds** | the person's login token | the person's login token | a private key (made here, never sent) plus a certificate the cluster signed (via a CSR) | a token the cluster issued for that ServiceAccount | no secret: only where to sign in; each person signs in in a browser | a client certificate and key in group `system:masters` |
| **the cluster sees** | `sso:alice@vsphere.local` | `sso:alice@vsphere.local` | `alice-<date>` | `system:serviceaccount:kubeconfig-access:cicd-account` | `keycloak-sv:<user>`, in `keycloak-sv:<group>` | `kubernetes-admin` |
| **rights** | what the admin bound to the name, or the person's group role on the vSphere Namespace (Can edit = cluster-admin) | same | `ROLE` (`edit` default, or `view`) in `VKS_CLUSTER_NAMESPACE`; "*" gives the whole cluster: cluster-admin with `edit`, read-only with `view` (WARNING) | `ROLE` (`edit` default, or `view`) in `VKS_CLUSTER_NAMESPACE`; "*" gives the whole cluster: cluster-admin with `edit`, read-only with `view` (WARNING) | `ROLE` (`edit` or `view`) for the group in `VKS_CLUSTER_NAMESPACE`; "*" gives the whole cluster: cluster-admin with `edit`, read-only with `view` (WARNING) | everything (`system:masters` bypasses RBAC) |
| **namespaces used** | `VSPHERE_NAMESPACE` | `VSPHERE_NAMESPACE` | `VKS_CLUSTER_NAMESPACE` (`"*"`: the whole cluster) | `SA_NAMESPACE` (where it lives), `VKS_CLUSTER_NAMESPACE` (rights; `"*"`: the whole cluster) | `VSPHERE_NAMESPACE` (export), `VKS_CLUSTER_NAMESPACE` (rights; `"*"`: the whole cluster) | `VSPHERE_NAMESPACE` (where the Secret is) |
| **valid for** | 10 hours per login | 10 hours per login | `DAYS` (120); cannot be revoked before that | `DAYS` (120), or until the ServiceAccount is deleted | binding: until deleted; sign-in: the identity-provider session | until the certificate's end date (the end date it prints) |
| **take it away** | delete the person's RoleBinding and their role on the vSphere Namespace | same | can't be revoked: delete its binding, never bind that name again | delete the ServiceAccount (with `"*"` also its ClusterRoleBinding): every copy answers Unauthorized within about 15 seconds | disable the person in the identity provider, or delete the group's binding | can't be taken from one holder: no binding to delete; a new Secret keeps old copies valid |
| **keeps working after take-away** | with `edit`: ServiceAccount tokens and workloads the person made (delete the workloads, re-create ServiceAccounts with rights, restart what uses them); with **Can edit** or **Owner**: also logins they made | same | with `edit`: ServiceAccount tokens and workloads the person made (delete the workloads, re-create ServiceAccounts with rights, restart what uses them); with `"*"` and `edit`: also logins they made | with `edit`: tokens made with `kubectl create token` and workloads it started (delete the workloads, re-create ServiceAccounts with rights, restart what uses them); with `"*"` and `edit`: logins made with it as a cluster administrator | with `edit`: ServiceAccount tokens and workloads members made (delete the workloads, re-create ServiceAccounts with rights, restart what uses them); with `"*"` and `edit`, or **Can edit**: also logins they made | everything: it cannot be taken away |
| **renew** | run again (password) | run again (password) | run again: a new name and certificate; the old one needs its own take-away | run again: a new token for the same ServiceAccount | nothing: each person signs in again | run again: the same file, or a newer certificate after the cluster rotates it |
| **meant for** | a person with a vCenter SSO account | a person without the VCF CLI | one person with no SSO or identity-provider account | tools, pipelines, or a team sharing one login | people in your identity provider with no vCenter account | the cluster's administrators only; the guides say not to send it |
| **needs on this host** | the VCF CLI and kubectl; the SSO password | kubectl-vsphere; the SSO password | admin kubeconfig `~/.kube/<cluster>.kubeconfig`, openssl | admin kubeconfig | admin kubeconfig (rights); an identity provider registered on the Supervisor (file; fails without one) | admin Supervisor login with Can edit or Owner (Can view: Forbidden) |
